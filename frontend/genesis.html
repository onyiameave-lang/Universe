Genesis Durable Control-Plane Architecture

Nexus owns the durable case workflow (`case_id`), Atlas builds the source-linked evidence dossier, Aegis governs preflight and permission boundaries, Genesis writes the versioned specification and invokes the isolated Bounded Coding Worker (Steps 0–3), Forge runs paired 6-suite evaluations on the frozen artifact hash, and Human release approval gates every canary deployment with tested Chronicle rollback.

1. Atlas Researches (Evidence Analyst) Builds source-linked dossier with claims, corroboration, contradiction, alternatives, and unresolved questions.
2. Nexus Coordinates (Workflow Owner) Owns case_id, deduplication, routing, and final choice among Repair, Extend, Tool, Create, or Defer.
3. Aegis Governs Risk (Risk Authority) Sets preflight limits before tool use and audits candidate permissions & security controls before release; can veto.
4. Genesis Creates (Agent Factory) Writes versioned specification, invokes the isolated Bounded Coding Worker (Steps 0–3), and never self-approves.
5. Forge Evaluates (Evaluation Lab) Runs paired 6-suite checks (baseline, validation, hidden holdout, integration, safety, regression) on frozen artifact hashes.
6. Chronicle Remembers (Durable Memory) Stores authoritative lifecycle records, policy versions, artifact hashes, human approvals, deployments, and rollbacks.
